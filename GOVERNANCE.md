# Governance

This project is maintained on a best-effort basis by its maintainers, who have
commit access and make the final calls on what gets merged and released.

## Maintainers

- [Micnubinub](https://github.com/Micnubinub)

## Decision making

- Maintainers decide by consensus. Where consensus cannot be reached, the
  oldest active maintainer breaks the tie.
- Small fixes (typos, dependency bumps, trivial refactors) may be merged
  directly.
- Larger changes go through review on the issue tracker or in a pull request,
  ideally with a chance for community comment.

## Roles

- **Contributors** submit issues and pull requests following [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).

## Becoming a maintainer

Contributors who show sustained, high quality involvement may be nominated by
an existing maintainer and approved by consensus of the maintainers.

## Releases

Releases are cut from the `main` branch and tagged with the app version
