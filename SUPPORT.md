# Support

## Where to get help

Please seek help on the forum and/or social media before creating an issue on
the tracker. Most questions are answered faster there.

## Wiki

The [wiki](../wiki) is the main knowledge base. Start there, especially for:

- [Switching from the deprecated official version](../wiki/migration/Switching-from-the-deprecated-official-version.md)
- Battery optimization and [persistent notification](../wiki/Persistent-Notification-required.md)
  notes
- Manufacturer specific workarounds
- Tips and tricks, including custom sync conditions and log retrieval

## Issue tracker

If the wiki and forum do not resolve your problem, open an issue on the
[tracker](https://github.com/Micnubinub/syncthing-android/issues). Include the
app version, Android version, device model, and relevant logs. See
[CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) for details.

## Security issues

See [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for reporting vulnerabilities privately.
